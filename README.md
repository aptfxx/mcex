## MCEX ≽(•⩊ •マ≼
A no-bullshit minecraft bedrock world identifier.  
Ever wanted to mess around with your level.dat? Ever been lost in the sea of folder name gibberish in the world directory and you have no clue which one is your world?  
MCEX finds all folders, displays their name and allows you to export it to a local folder for your tampering.  

## Usage ˖˚⊹ ꣑ৎ‎

When you launch MCEX, and you have more than one profile on your account, you will choose which one it is. Choose the right account and view the worlds there.  
When you select the world, it will be exported to \mcex\exports\world_name in the directory where you installed mcex.  
For some people, you may have an old world stored in AppData\Local. When there is no installation in AppData\Roaming, you will be asked if you want to enter "Legacy Mode".  

## What's Different

From what I know, there is no tool like this, it speeds up accessing your world files tenfold. It's a very specific use case, but will save a lot of time if you have hundreds of worlds to sort through and find the `levelname.txt`.
